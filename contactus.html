Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the admission procedure for new students?

Admissions are open throughout the academic year. Parents can visit the school office with the child's birth certificate and previous school leaving certificate.

Are the smart digital classes available for all grades?

Yes, all 67 students across 1st to 7th standard have access to interactive digital TV classes and audio-visual educational modules.

How is student security maintained on campus?

The entire school campus, entry gates, and corridors are monitored 24/7 by high-definition CCTV cameras with trained school staff supervision.
