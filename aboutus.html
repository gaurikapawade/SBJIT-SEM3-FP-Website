Our Heritage & Journey

Nurturing Rural Talent in Yerla Village

Zilla Parishad Upper Primary School, Yerla is located in Kalameshwar Block, District Nagpur, Maharashtra. Established with a profound dedication to providing high-quality, inclusive, and accessible elementary education to every child in the community.

Over the years, our school has evolved into a vibrant educational sanctuary. We combine traditional Maharashtrian cultural values with modern technology—featuring Interactive Smart Digital TVs, 24/7 CCTV security surveillance, pure drinking water facilities, and expansive green playgrounds.
