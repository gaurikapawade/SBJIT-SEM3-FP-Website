Campus Infrastructure

Modern, Safe & Inspiring Facilities

Discover the key campus facilities providing a safe, digital, and healthy environment for every child.

Security & Safety

24/7 CCTV Camera Security

High-definition 24/7 CCTV cameras monitor gates, corridors, and classrooms to ensure complete child safety and peace of mind.

Accessibility & Roads

Good Transportation Roads

Wide, paved asphalt roads connect Yerla village directly to main highway corridors for easy, safe daily commuting.

Smart Education

Digital TV in Classrooms

Interactive Smart TVs in classrooms bring lessons to life with educational videos and animated visual learning modules.

Eco Learning

Open-Air Eco Classrooms

Shady tree-filled open classrooms offer fresh air for interactive group discussions, storytelling, and practical nature study.

Sports & Fitness

Huge Sports Playground

A spacious open playground dedicated to Kho-Kho, Kabaddi, Volleyball, athletics, and morning fitness drills.

Health & Hygiene

24-Hour Pure Water Supply

Overhead water storage tank and filtration system providing continuous purified drinking water and hygienic washrooms.
